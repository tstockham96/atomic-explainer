# Murph clean dial texture

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, using the manufacturer front photograph and dial macro as reference inputs. The output is a material on a thin 3D disc; it is never substituted for the entire watch model. The selected local artifact is `work/murph-authored/dial-clean.png` in the parent workspace.

## Final prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: clean base-color texture for a physically separate 3D watch dial. Reference 1 is the exact front Hamilton Khaki Field Murph 42mm H70605731; reference 2 gives macro printing/material details. Produce ONLY its isolated flat circular black dial, orthographic straight-on, perfectly circular and centered, fills the entire square canvas edge-to-edge (circle touches the midpoint of all four edges). Outside circle also black. Remove ALL three hands, pinion/cap, all shadows of hands, glass reflections, bezel, case, crown, strap. Reconstruct the actual unoccluded smooth matte black surface under those hands, not black hand-shaped patches. Keep the exact creamy pale beige sans-serif wide distinctive Arabic numerals 1 through 12, correct orientations and positions, outer small minute numerals 5,10,15,20,25,30,35,40,45,50,55,60, minute and subdivision ticks. Text exactly HAMILTON in its distinctive font centered above central pivot; KHAKI and AUTOMATIC on two lines below pivot; tiny SWISS MADE at bottom. No extra lettering, no extra indices. No center hole, no lighting gradient, no fake dimensional bevels, no photographic perspective. This is a uniform base color/albedo texture, so all reflections, shadows, metallic parts and thickness will come from the separate 3D renderer. Very high precision crisp printed markings, subtle matte grain. The distinctive Arabic 4 is open, and 1 is simple vertical stroke. No vintage patina. Match the front reference closely.

## Inspection

All three hands and their shadows are absent; all twelve hour numerals and principal text are readable. A small misplaced tick near the 40-minute region remains. The texture is a generated reconstruction, not a factory dial drawing or a new source of evidence.
