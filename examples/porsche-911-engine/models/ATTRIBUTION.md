# Model provenance

`engine.glb` is an original educational reconstruction generated by `tools/porsche-study/model.ts` and `tools/porsche-study/build.mjs`, licensed under the repository's MIT license. No downloaded third-party meshes or photograph textures are embedded.

Research references are listed in `../research.json`; those sources retain their own rights. Reference diagrams informed architecture and part shapes. Exact dimensional and material fidelity is not established.
