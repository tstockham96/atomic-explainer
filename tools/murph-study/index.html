01 / Protection

A window grown from crystal.

The front sapphire protects the dial without hiding it. Synthetic sapphire starts with alumina; crystal growth, precision shaping and polishing turn it into an optical component. This describes the material’s general manufacturing process.

02 / Display

Light held in a thin outline.

The cathedral outlines give the hands their identity. Metal supports a luminous fill, making the hour and minute readable in the dark. Stamping, finishing and applying luminous material are distinct operations.

03 / Energy

Your wrist winds the story.

The rotor turns around its bearing as the watch moves. A winding mechanism uses that motion to replenish the mainspring. The spring stores energy; the balance and escapement regulate its release.

About this reconstruction and its evidence

This is an educational reconstruction of the Hamilton Khaki Field Murph 42 mm, not manufacturer CAD. The dial artwork was regenerated without hands. Case dimensions and visible design follow manufacturer specifications and front, side and rear references. The rotor, bridges and mechanism are independently authored geometry informed by Hamilton movement references and close-up photographs of the actual Murph. Unpublished internal dimensions, hidden layout, gear ratios and oscillation timing are approximate; this is not a working movement simulation. The rear window is transparent, but its precise material is unverified. The exterior case and leather retain the earlier Tripo reconstruction; the case interior has been opened for the independent assembly.

  • Hamilton: model specifications and photographs
  • Hamilton: H-10 family movement reference
  • Fratello: original Murph, including a rear view
  • SJX: Murph movement close-ups from several angles
