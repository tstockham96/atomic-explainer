# Workshop scenery — CC0 1.0

These are decorative environment assets, not research evidence or product anatomy.
Downloaded and inspected 2026-11-02. Original GLBs are retained in `tools/workshop-scene/assets/`. Their geometry is included in the baked `../atelier/workshop.glb`; only the assembled scene is loaded at runtime, with no third-party requests.

## Desk Lamp Arm 01

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/desk_lamp_arm_01
Modeling/texturing: Kuutti Siitonen. Rigging: Yann Kervran.
License: CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license

The official 1K glTF, binary buffer and JPEG maps were combined into `desk-lamp.glb` without changing geometry or image data. Blender authoring places and scales the prop in the complete room, then bakes its appearance under the shared lighting. This is the source model's static pose, not an interactive lamp rig.

## Workbench, Bench Vise, Vernier Caliper

Creator: Grab3D. Cozy Tools & Workshop Kit.
Sources:
- https://grab3d.com/models/tools/workbench/
- https://grab3d.com/models/tools/bench-vise/
- https://grab3d.com/models/tools/vernier-caliper/

Original GLBs are unmodified. Blender orientation, scale, materials and shared room lighting are adjusted to fit the set before baking. Grab3D discloses that these assets were created with procedural Blender scripts written with AI coding assistance; they are not scans. See `Grab3D-LICENSE.txt` for the original disclosure and CC0 dedication, verified for all three files.

`CC0-1.0.txt` contains the license text. `manifest.json` records original source byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes. The room shell, mat artwork and remaining props are authored by this project. Furniture proportions are art direction, not physical scale evidence for the inspected object.
