# Explode workshop scene

An authored, lit and baked Blender scene. Original room design and lighting: Explode contributors (project MIT license).

Includes derivative geometry from CC0 sources: Poly Haven Desk Lamp Arm 01 (Kuutti Siitonen, rigging Yann Kervran), and Grab3D Workbench, Bench Vise and Vernier Caliper. Full source URLs, origin disclosures, license texts and original file hashes are retained in `../workshop/`.

The baked room, bench, props and mat textures and HDR probe are rendered from this scene. They are bundled locally in every exported study. No Bruno Simon, Framer or Spline assets are included.

Rebuild and edit: `tools/workshop-scene/` in the Explode repository.
