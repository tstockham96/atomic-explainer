# Hamilton Khaki Field Murph educational reconstruction

`murph.glb` is the project's authored/generated educational study asset, supplied under the repository's MIT license. It is not Hamilton CAD, an official Hamilton asset, or a mechanically accurate H-10 simulation. Hamilton names and markings identify the subject; trademark rights are not granted by the code license.

- Independent crystal, hands, dial, caseback, rear window, rotor and internal assemblies are authored by this project. Their source is `src/authoring/`.
- The exterior case and strap retain portions of an earlier Tripo-generated reconstruction. The dial artwork was generated separately without hands. These retain visible approximation and texture errors.
- Research sources and uncertainties: `docs/research/murph-study.json`, `docs/research/murph-material-review.md`. Original Hamilton, Fratello and SJX photographs are referenced, not distributed in this folder.
- The exported asset embeds its textures and component metadata. The original photo/generation intermediates are not required to run it; rebuilding those exterior source surfaces requires new source inputs.
- `studio-small-09.hdr`: **Studio Small 09**, Sergej Majboroda, Poly Haven, **CC0**. https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_09 . This is lighting, not watch geometry.

All 16 assemblies can be inspected. The researched buckle has no independent model. Hidden mechanisms, dimensions, hand backs and unknown materials remain educational approximations. Consult the evaluation report before making fidelity claims.
