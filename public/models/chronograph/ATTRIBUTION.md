# Chronograph Watch

Model and textures by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH, 2025.
Derived from **Chronograph Watch Mudmaster** by **graphiccompressor**: https://skfb.ly/oAsPA

Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/ChronographWatch
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The GLB is retained without modification. Atomic Explainer adds runtime grouping, lighting, camera choreography, animation playback, and exploded transforms. The originally planar glass cover receives an illustrative thin beveled edge generated from its own boundary at runtime; its thickness is not a manufacturer measurement. The rear plate is rendered from both sides during the teardown. This is a different model from the Hamilton Murph; no Hamilton accuracy is claimed. It contains no internal watch movement.
